PUN OPIS:
On the motorway and need fuel, a coffee, a toilet or a break? Motorway Info shows what's ahead on every motorway in Great Britain and Northern Ireland – junction by junction, sorted by mile, in your direction of travel.

Pick a motorway and your direction, and you get:
• every junction and interchange with its mile
• motorway services and rest areas on your side of the road
• fuel stations with brand logos, restaurants and facilities (toilets, showers, shop, hotel)
• EV chargers and HGV parking
• phone number and website where available

DRIVING MODE
Start Driving mode and the app finds the motorway you are on, the mile and your direction, and shows the next junctions, services and fuel stations ahead. When you change motorway, the list follows you. Stop it with one tap or by closing the app.

TRAFFIC NEWS
Road and lane closures, roadworks and incidents from National Highways (England), Traffic Wales and Northern Ireland's Traffic Information and Control Centre, with direction. With notifications on, you get alerts for the motorway you selected or the one you are driving on. Scotland coming soon.

COVERAGE
32 motorways: M1, M4, M5, M6, M25, M40, M62, M8, M74, A1(M) and more, plus the M1 and M2 in Northern Ireland.

WORKS OFFLINE
All motorway data is built into the app, so the list works without signal. Only traffic news needs a connection.

MADE FOR DRIVERS
• one tap opens navigation to the place
• share any services or junction
• filters: fuel, food, parking, junctions, EV charging
• no account, no sign-up; your location stays on your phone
• one-time purchase to remove ads
• 18 languages
